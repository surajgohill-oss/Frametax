The full combination count of this budget was not measured (the runs were aborted); one unprofiled evaluation takes about 251 s inside the memo scope.
- The stale-planner-statistics hypothesis is refuted for this slowdown (the earlier stall was a single long SELECT on a transaction that had accumulated nine evaluations; that is a separate, now avoided, pattern).
