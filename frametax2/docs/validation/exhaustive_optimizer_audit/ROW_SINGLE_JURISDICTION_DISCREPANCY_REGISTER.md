## 2. Detailed Adjudication of Active Code Defects (`canonical-1.122.0`)

### `ROW-DEF-001` — Spain Foreign Production Tax Credit (`es_tax_credit_foreign`, Ley 27/2014 Art. 36.2 LIS): Category Omission, False Minimum-Spend Rejection on `FVD`, and Improper Unallocated Contingency Inclusion

- **Primary Authority:** *Ley 27/2014, de 27 de noviembre, del Impuesto sobre Sociedades (BOE-A-2014-12328), Artículo 36.2* (as amended by Ley 38/2022):
  - **Rate & Cap:** `30%` on the first `EUR 1,000,000` (`$1,140,523.96 USD` at `0.87679 EUR/USD`) of qualifying Spanish expenditure and `25%` on the excess, up to `EUR 20,000,000` (`$22,810,479.13 USD`) per feature film.
  - **Minimum Qualifying Spanish Expenditure:** `EUR 1,000,000` (`$1,140,523.96 USD`) for live-action feature films (`EUR 200,000` for animation).
  - **Eligible Base (Art. 36.2.a LIS):**
    1. *Category 1 (`Art. 36.2.a.1º`):* Creative personnel (*personal creativo*), provided they have tax residence in Spain or another EEA Member State, capped at `EUR 100,000` per person.
    2. *Category 2 (`Art. 36.2.a.2º`):* Expenses derived from the utilization of technical industries and other suppliers (*industrias técnicas y otros proveedores*) in Spanish territory (all BTL crew, camera, lighting/electric, wardrobe, makeup/hair, art/props, picture vehicles, SFX, production sound, locations, stages, construction, transportation, post/VFX/music, payroll fringes, insurance, and travel/lodging).
- **Frozen Engine Call Path & Defect (`canonical-1.122.0`):**
  - In [`backend/app/data/program_spend_rules.py:249`](file:///Users/Suraj/cineglobe-ag-exhaustive-optimizer-audit/frametax2/backend/app/data/program_spend_rules.py#L249) and [`782-795`](file:///Users/Suraj/cineglobe-ag-exhaustive-optimizer-audit/frametax2/backend/app/data/program_spend_rules.py#L782-L795), `es_tax_credit_foreign` is classified as `HYBRID_CONDITIONAL`. Under `HYBRID_CONDITIONAL`, any `SpendCategory` not explicitly listed in `ES_RULES` defaults to `qualifies=None` (`GREY_AREA_REQUIRES_AUTHORITY`), which contributes `$0.00` to `qpe_usd`.
  - `ES_RULES` **omits** `miscellaneous`, `production_sound`, and `general_administration`. On `FVD`, `BH`, and `LLS`, the budget parser mapped core BTL technical departments (`2000 PRODUCTION STAFF`, `2100 EXTRA TALENT`, `2200 ART DIRECTION`, `2500/2800 PROPERTY`, `2600/2900 PICTURE VEHICLES & ANIMALS`, `2700/3000 WARDROBE`, `3000/3200 ELECTRIC/LIGHTING`, `3100/3300 CAMERA`, `3400/3600 LOCATIONS`, `3800 PRODUCTION FILM & LAB`, `3900 2ND UNIT`) to `spend_category = "miscellaneous"` and `PRODUCTION SOUND` (`2600`/`3200`/`3400`) to `spend_category = "production_sound"`.
  - Consequently, `ES_RULES` silently drops **`$899,254.00`** of BTL technical spend on `FVD`, **`$468,346.00`** on `BH`, **`$2,556,576.00`** on `LLS`, and **`$69,532.00`** (`3200 PRODUCTION SOUND`) on `LU`.
  - Simultaneously, [`backend/app/data/program_spend_rules.py:790`](file:///Users/Suraj/cineglobe-ag-exhaustive-optimizer-audit/frametax2/backend/app/data/program_spend_rules.py#L790) defines `_es_tech("contingency")` (`qualifies=True`), causing `apply_contingency_policy` on `LU` (`contingency_inclusion_election = 100_PCT`) to add `$301,131.00` of unallocated contingency reserve directly into confirmed `qpe_usd` (`$3,819,151.00 + $301,131.00 = $4,120,282.00`).
