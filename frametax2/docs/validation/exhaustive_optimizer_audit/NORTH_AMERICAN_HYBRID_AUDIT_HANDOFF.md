3. **Three-Way Distinction Among Domestic CPTC (`ITR § 1106(4)`), Treaty Co-Production CPTC (`ITR § 1106(3)`), and Federal PSTC (`ITA § 125.5`):** Domestic CAVCO `6/10` Canadian creative-point requirements (`Income Tax Regulations § 1106(4)`) apply **only** to domestic `ca_federal_cptc`, **never** to official bilateral treaty co-productions (`ITR § 1106(3)`) and **never** to the foreign/service `ca_federal_pstc` (`ITA § 125.5`, `ITR § 9300`). Any prior audit phrasing that applied domestic `6/10` CAVCO creative points to treaty co-productions is **explicitly withdrawn**.
4. **Rejected Candidates Are Not Priced (`N/A (NOT_PRICED_REJECTED_CANDIDATE)`):** Whenever a hybrid or component-relocation candidate is rejected (`GATE_REJECTED`, `RULE_REJECTED`, `CONDITIONAL_NONDETERMINISTIC`, including `LLS` Manitoba–California Artifact 1), its incentive floor and NPC columns are recorded as `N/A (NOT_PRICED_REJECTED_CANDIDATE)` rather than a misleading `$0.00` credit with `NPC = Gross Budget`.
5. **Contingency Election vs. Eligible Category Deployment:** Under Manitoba ITA s. 7.5(1)(g)–(h), a user's top-level `contingency_expected_utilization_pct` election (`50%` or `100%`) reduces undeployed planned cost once (`planned_cost_reduction_usd`) but is **not by itself evidence that the deployed reserve was spent on eligible Manitoba production categories**. Unallocated contingency is kept out of the confirmed QPE floor (`$2,833,096.20` on Artifact 2; `$2,845,096.20` on Artifact 3), while deployed-to-eligible-category contingency (`+$60,000` at `50%`, `+$120,000` at `100%`) is presented as a conditional deployment sensitivity scenario.
6. **No Automatic Classification of BTL Department Totals as Labour:** Movie Magic topsheet department totals (`2000–3900`) bundle crew labour, equipment rentals, and materials and are never automatically treated as 100% labour.

### 2.2 Three-Route Statutory Distinction: CAVCO Certification vs. Domestic CPTC (`ITR § 1106(4)`) vs. Treaty Co-Production CPTC (`ITR § 1106(3)`) vs. Federal PSTC (`ITA § 125.5`)

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Formal Withdrawal of Conflated Creative-Point Requirement on Treaty Co-Productions:**
> Any statement in earlier audit notes suggesting that Canadian treaty co-productions require `>= 6/10` domestic CAVCO Canadian creative points is **formally withdrawn**. Under the Canadian *Income Tax Act* (R.S.C. 1985, c. 1 (5th Supp.)) and *Income Tax Regulations* (C.R.C., c. 945), CAVCO administers three legally distinct certification routes:

| **Route 1: Domestic CPTC** (`ca_federal_cptc` — Domestic Production) | **ITA § 125.4** & **Income Tax Regulations § 1106(4)** (`"Canadian film or video production"`) | **CAVCO Part A / Part B Canadian Film or Video Production Certificate** | **100% Canadian copyright ownership** (`ITR § 1106(4)(a)`) AND **`>= 6/10` CAVCO Canadian creative points** (lead director/screenwriter Canadian + at least 1 of top 2 lead performers Canadian; `ITR § 1106(4)(b)` & `(5)`). | **`>= 75%` of total non-remuneration production services** paid to Canadians AND **`>= 75%` of post-production/lab costs** incurred in Canada (`ITR § 1106(4)(c)–(d)`). | **25% of Qualified Labour Expenditure (QLE)**, where QLE is capped at **60% of net production cost** (net of all federal/provincial assistance; max **15%** of net production cost).
