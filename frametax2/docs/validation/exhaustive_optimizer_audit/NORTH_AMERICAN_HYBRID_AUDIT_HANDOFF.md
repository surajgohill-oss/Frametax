## 1. Executive Summary & Audit Methodology

In accordance with `PROJECT_RULES.md`, `docs/architecture/CAPABILITY_LEDGER.md`, `docs/validation/FOUR_ANCHOR_QPE_ISSUE_REGISTER.md`, and Codex's `CODEX_CA_GATE_REVIEW_20261011.md`, `CODEX_LLS_NA_HYBRID_LOAD_REVIEW_20261011.md`, and `CODEX_NY_CANADA_CHANGED_PATH_REVIEW_20261011.md`, this audit independently reconstructs the complete North American hybrid universe across all four anchor projects (`The Little Utopia` [`LU`], `F#K Valentine's Day` [`FVD`], `Bad Hombres` [`BH`], and `Lips Like Sugar` [`LLS`]) outside the engine from retained Movie Magic budget lines (`docs/validation/FOUR_ANCHOR_SOURCE_DETAIL_EVIDENCE.json`) and dated primary statutory authority.

### Core Methodological Controls Enforced
1. **Independent Expectation First by Exact Account Identity (Zero Circularity):** Every segment allocation (`37` exact line-identity partitions across the four budgets), eligibility gate, QPE/labour base, statutory cap, uplift, government assistance deduction, line overlap (`0` shared `line_id`s across all partitions and saved hybrids), contingency treatment, and Net Production Cost (NPC) was calculated directly from `FOUR_ANCHOR_SOURCE_DETAIL_EVIDENCE.json` and primary statute/guidelines without reading engine QPE or incentive outputs.
2. **Rejected Candidates Are Not Priced (`N/A (NOT_PRICED_REJECTED_CANDIDATE)`):** Whenever a hybrid or component-relocation candidate is rejected (`GATE_REJECTED`, `RULE_REJECTED`, `CONDITIONAL_NONDETERMINISTIC`, including `LLS` Manitoba–California Artifact 1), its incentive floor and NPC columns are recorded as `N/A (NOT_PRICED_REJECTED_CANDIDATE)` rather than a misleading `$0.00` credit with `NPC = Gross Budget`.
3. **Contingency Election vs. Eligible Category Deployment:** Under Manitoba ITA s. 7.5(1)(g)–(h), a user's top-level `contingency_expected_utilization_pct` election (`50%` or `100%`) reduces undeployed planned cost once (`planned_cost_reduction_usd`) but is **not by itself evidence that the deployed reserve was spent on eligible Manitoba production categories**. Unallocated contingency is kept out of the confirmed QPE floor (`$2,833,096.20` on Artifact 2; `$2,845,096.20` on Artifact 3), while deployed-to-eligible-category contingency (`+$60,000` at `50%`, `+$120,000` at `100%`) is presented as a conditional deployment sensitivity scenario.
4. **No Automatic Classification of BTL Department Totals as Labour:** Movie Magic topsheet department totals (`2000–3900`) bundle crew labour, equipment rentals, and materials and are never automatically treated as 100% labour.
