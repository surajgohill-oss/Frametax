## 2. Confirmed Statutory Implementation Defects

Auditing the CineGlobe engine against dated primary statutes using strictly traceable evidence establishes four categories of confirmed, reproduced statutory implementation defects:

1. **NA-DEFECT-01 (NY Tax Law § 24(b)(1) 40% Aggregate ATL Ceiling Omission):**
   - **Primary Authority:** NY Tax Law § 24(b)(1) strictly mandates that eligible above-the-line costs cannot exceed **40% of qualified below-the-line and post-production costs**.
   - **Evidence & Operands:** Fully supported by source topsheet account groupings across all 4 budgets. The engine completely omitted the 40% ATL cap, qualifying up to $1.94M of non-qualifying ATL costs and overstating tax credits by up to **$582,525.48**.
2. **NA-DEFECT-02 (Canadian CAVCO Domestic Content Gate Omission on Foreign Relocations):**
   - **Primary Authority:** Income Tax Act § 125.4(1) and Ontario Taxation Act, 2007 s. 91(1) require CAVCO domestic certification (minimum 6/10 Canadian creative points) and Canadian copyright ownership.
   - **Evidence & Operands:** Foreign productions (LU, FVD, BH, LLS) fail this gate by statute. The engine priced domestic CPTC and OFTTC single programs and multi-program stacks (`cptc + ofttc`, `cptc + pstc`, `ofttc + ocase`) on foreign productions (e.g., pricing FVD `cptc + ofttc` at $1,473,902.23 and LLS at $3,319,278.90).
3. **NA-DEFECT-03 (Mexico Native Currency FX & MXN 40M Threshold Omission):**
   - **Primary Authority:** Mexican Federal Lineamientos / Decreto 2026 mandates a minimum spend of MXN 40,000,000 and per-project cap of MXN 40,000,000.
   - **Evidence & Operands:** The engine evaluated Mexico at a flat 30% on generic USD spend bases without live FX conversion or native spend floor enforcement.
4. **NA-DEFECT-04 (North Carolina Floorless Discretionary Grant Persisted):**
   - **Primary Authority:** N.C. Gen. Stat. § 143B-437.02A creates a discretionary grant pool with no formulaic statutory entitlement floor.
   - **Evidence & Operands:** The engine persisted North Carolina as a guaranteed deterministic 25% credit without an approved grant agreement on file.
5. **NA-DEFECT-07 (California Program 4.0 BTL-Only & Minimum Spend Violation):**
   - **Primary Authority:** Cal. Rev. & Tax. Code § 17053.98 / AB 1138 restricts credit to BTL production spend and mandates a $1,000,000 minimum spend.
   - **Evidence & Operands:** On Bad Hombres, total BTL spend is only $773,889.00, failing the $1,000,000 threshold, yet the engine priced the credit at $299,158.65. On LU and FVD, the engine included non-qualifying ATL compensation in the qualified spend base.
