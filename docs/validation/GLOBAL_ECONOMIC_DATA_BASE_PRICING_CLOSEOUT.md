## Why `BLOCKED`, not `ACCEPTED`

of 126 formulaic programs individually resolved to `PRICED` or a
genuinely-exhausted `ECONOMIC_INPUT_GENUINELY_MISSING` boundary (84

not a failure of this session's method). What this session

a report: 10 real, government-sourced, runtime-verified formulaic prices,
a validated recover-before-research method that already found 73 of 94
blocked programs have reusable prior work, and a precisely itemized,
prioritized workload for the next research batch.
