## DETAILED FINDINGS

### 1. Canada: British Columbia (PSTC)
*   **Issue:** Incomplete/Inaccurate Modeling
*   **Description:** CineGlobe’s application of the Digital Animation, Visual Effects and Post-Production (DAVE) tax credit is often applied too broadly as a flat 16% on all post-production. Authoritative guidelines explicitly state DAVE applies specifically to *eligible BC labor directly attributable to digital animation or VFX activities*, not general post-production.
*   **Authority:** Creative BC PSTC Guidelines
*   **Required Remediation:** Ensure DAVE is restricted strictly to eligible BC digital animation/VFX labor.

### 2. United States: Georgia
*   **Issue:** Incorrect / Stale Modeling
*   **Description:** The Georgia Entertainment Industry Investment Act recently underwent legislative changes. CineGlobe is missing the new **$500,000 cap** on qualifying compensation per individual for Above-The-Line (ATL) personnel.
*   **Authority:** Georgia Department of Economic Development / HB 1180 legislative text.
*   **Required Remediation:** Implement a per-individual $500k ATL cap on QPE.

### 3. Hungary (MISSING)
*   **Issue:** Material Missing Program
*   **Description:** The Hungarian Film Incentive offers an effective 30% rebate on eligible Hungarian spend. Crucially, non-Hungarian eligible spend can qualify up to 25% of the Hungarian eligible spend. This effectively allows the 30% rebate to apply to 1.25x the local spend. This is a massive omission for European optimization.
*   **Authority:** National Film Institute Hungary (NFI)
*   **Required Remediation:** Add Hungary base program and the 25% foreign spend inclusion rule.

### 4. Spain: Canary Islands (MISSING)
*   **Issue:** Material Missing Sub-Jurisdiction Uplift
*   **Description:** While mainland Spain offers 30%, the Canary Islands offer a highly competitive 54% on the first €1 million and 45% thereafter, with a maximum deduction of €36 million per feature film.
*   **Authority:** Canary Islands Film Commission / Spanish Corporate Tax Law
*   **Required Remediation:** Add Canary Islands distinct tax credit (54%/45%) with €36M cap.

*   **Required Remediation:** Complete mapping of major bilateral treaties for CA, UK, AU, and FR.
