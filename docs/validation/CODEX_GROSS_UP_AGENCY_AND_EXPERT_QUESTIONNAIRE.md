# Codex Gross-Up Agency and Expert Questionnaire

Use only after identifying the exact program and proposed transaction. A category ceiling is not permission to create expenditure.

## Ireland Section 481 — ruling questions

1. If a qualifying company pays a substantiated arm’s-length producer fee for real services no later than four months after completion, and the producer subsequently subscribes that cash as equity in a legally separate transaction, does the original fee remain eligible expenditure? Identify the statutory/regulatory basis and required separation in time, documents, bank flows, corporate approvals, tax reporting, and beneficial ownership.
2. Is contribution or capitalization of a producer-fee receivable treated as “payment” for Regulation 12, or must cash move? If non-cash settlement can qualify, what valuation and evidence are required?
3. Does a loan-funded fee payment qualify where the producer or qualifying company retains full repayment liability? What loan-party, recourse, interest, maturity, security, and source-of-funds facts affect the answer?
4. How do credit notes, waivers, discounts, later forgiveness, set-off, contra entries, vendor rebates, or capital contributions alter total cost, eligible expenditure, and any previously claimed credit?
5. Do the 15%/10% producer-fee parameters apply to the final global budget after the fee, and how is the 80% eligible-spend threshold tested when the fee changes both numerator and denominator?
6. Which exact records must accompany a ruling or compliance review: service contract, producer-company cost build-up, timesheets/deliverables, invoice, payroll/VAT/withholding returns, bank statements, general ledger, related-party schedule, board/share subscription documents, loan documents, payable aging, credit notes, and final cost report?

## Reusable program questionnaire

For each archetype A-K, request a separate yes/no/conditional answer. Require the authority to distinguish incurred from paid, state the payment deadline, define arm’s-length/FMV treatment, identify assistance and forgiveness adjustments, address same-cost/double-counting rules, state whether non-cash settlement is payment, and list audit evidence. If the answer depends on facts or discretion, request a written ruling and do not translate silence into optimizer permission.

## Professional-practice evidence request

Ask an experienced incentive CPA or production accountant for an anonymized, authority-reviewed example containing the original budget, final cost report, fee calculation, signed obligation, payable aging, proof of payment, any equity subscription, loan liability, credit notes/audit adjustments, certified QPE, and final incentive. Practice evidence may demonstrate mechanics but cannot override the administering authority.
